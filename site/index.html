Watch your robot clean, in 3D.

A Home Assistant integration for Dreame robot vacuums, with a card that draws the robot moving through a 3D model of your house: its path, the floor it vacuums and mops, and what it finds on the way.

Add to Home Assistant Read the docs

Your own house, in 3D

Load a model of your house and say where the dock is: the card puts the robot, its path and the rooms from the robot's map on it. Draw it as it is, with its textures, or as a hologram that keeps the shapes.

No model yet? The card draws the robot's own map, and the repository has the prompt an AI agent follows to build one from phone photos, on the floor plan the robot made.

What the card shows

Everything comes from the robot's own map frames, live while it cleans.

  • The robot and its path

    Where it is, which way it faces, the path it took and the rooms it is cleaning, with the room it is in on the card and as a sensor.

  • Vacuumed and mopped floor

    Where it vacuums, the dust clears and the floor glints; where it mops, the floor turns dark and glossy and dries out over the next minutes.

  • What it finds on the floor

    Shoes, socks, cables, pet waste or spills, where its camera saw them, and anything the lidar sees on floor that used to be open.

  • Rooms you can tap

    Tap a room to clean it, or save routines on the card: rooms, suction, water and passes in one button.

Local control, the cloud only for the map

Commands stay on your network

Start, stop, rooms, suction and every setting go straight to the robot over miIO. The Xiaomi cloud is only a fallback.

The map from Xiaomi's cloud

The robot uploads its map frames there, so the live map needs your Xiaomi Home account. Two-factor sign-in is supported.

Your automations keep working

Its cleaning services take the same names and fields as Tasshack's integration: moving an automation over is a find and replace.

Install it

  1. Add the integration with HACS

    Open it in HACS, choose Download and restart Home Assistant. Without HACS, copy it from the latest release.

  2. Sign in and pick your robot

    In Settings → Devices & services, add Dreame Vacuum 3D and sign in with your Xiaomi Home account. It is tested on the Xiaomi X10+; other Dreame robots get the entities for what they report, untested so far (models).

  3. Add the card to a dashboard

    The integration serves the card itself. Search for Dreame Vacuum 3D Card in Add card, or paste this:

    type: custom:dreame-vacuum-3d-card
entity: vacuum.my_robot
routines:
  - name: After dinner
    rooms: [Kitchen, Living room]
